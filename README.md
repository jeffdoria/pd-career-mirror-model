## What this is

This repo is an interactive, bilingual (English / Portuguese) study version of the Mirror Model. I built it because English isn't my first language, and I wanted a way to go through the model zone by zone without losing the original terms or taking anything out of context.

- **Original charts**: Ryan Ford's Mirror Model chart (top of the page) and his "Current Startup Product Design Career Path" chart are shown with credit. Click any chart to zoom in; each caption links to the full-resolution file. The images belong to him.
- **Startup and enterprise paths**: two extra sections explain why the current paths are broken, before the zones of the Mirror Model.
- **Horizontal navigation**: one panel per zone, from the shared early career (Zone 1) to the Chief Design Officer (Zone 6). Use the buttons, the section rail, swipe, or the ← → keys.
- **English / Portuguese switch**: level names and zone titles follow the original chart. Descriptions are short summaries, not the original text.
- **Deep links**: each section has its own address, e.g. `#zone-4`.
- **Accessibility**: WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, 48px touch targets, `lang` on every text block, reduced motion support.
- **Layout**: 8-point spacing grid and a white background, using colors close to the original chart.

## The model in one paragraph

Product design career paths tend to cap individual contributors at the Principal level, so designers who love the craft have to move into management to keep growing. Using engineering career paths as a template, the Mirror Model adds **Distinguished** and **Fellow** levels to the creative track, so that it mirrors the management track level for level, and both can lead to Chief Design Officer.

| Zone | Creative path (IC) | Management path (M) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | IC-1/IC-2 Internships · IC-3 Jr/Associate PD · IC-4 Product Designer | (shared) |
| 2 | IC-5 Senior PD · IC-6 Lead/Staff PD | M-1 Senior PD · M-2 Lead/Staff PD |
| 3 | IC-7 Principal PD · IC-8 Sr Principal PD | M-3 Manager · M-4 Sr Manager |
| 4 | IC-D1 Distinguished PD · IC-D2 Sr Distinguished PD | M-D1 Director · M-D2 Sr Director |
| 5 | IC-F1 PD Fellow · IC-F2 Sr PD Fellow · IC-F3 Exec. PD Fellow | M-VP1 VP · M-VP2 Sr VP · M-VP3 Exec. VP |
| 6 | CDO, Chief Design Officer | CDO, Chief Design Officer |

## Run it

It's a single static file. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static host (GitHub Pages, Vercel, etc.).
